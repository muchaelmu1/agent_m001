How a task moves

Every task is either finished or handed off — never left ambiguous

Serveai agents don't guess when they're unsure. A task moves through four states, and you can see which one it's in at any moment.

01 — received

Task lands

From a ticket, an inbox, an inventory alert, or a scheduled check.

02 — working

Agent acts

Reads context across your connected systems, takes the action policy allows.

03 — resolved

Closed and logged

Customer notified, record updated, full trace kept for audit.

03b — escalated

Handed to a person

When judgment or exception approval is needed — with full context attached.

!

Escalation isn't a failure state. Agents are configured with the exact boundaries of what they can decide on their own. Anything outside that boundary — a policy exception, an angry customer, an unusual number — goes to a person with the full trail attached, not a dead end.
